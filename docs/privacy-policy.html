Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

MileOwl tracks your mileage. That's it. Everything stays on your phone — we never see your data.

What MileOwl uses

Your location — to record where you drive and how far. This runs in the background so your trips get tracked even when you're not looking at the app.

Motion detection — to figure out when you start and stop driving, so tracking happens automatically.

Both of these stay on your phone. Nothing gets sent anywhere.

What we don't do

We don't ask you to create an account. We don't run ads. We don't use analytics. We don't send your data to any server. We don't share or sell anything to anyone. MileOwl doesn't even connect to the internet.

Your data, your control

You can export your trips as a spreadsheet anytime. If you delete the app, everything goes with it. There's nothing on our end to delete because we never had it.

Kids

MileOwl isn't built for kids under 13.

Changes

If anything here changes, we'll update this page. Since we don't collect data, that's unlikely.
